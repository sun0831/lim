## 핵심
- 86 is not a count of new primitives.
- SKILL_RECLASSIFY fragments can reuse the existing primitive.
- target selection, condition, action trigger, status/effect, initial resource state, coin/power transform and skill transform belong to existing/common axes and should not create a generic non_resource runtime.
- 26 clauses remain unresolved because they are incomplete fragments, compound reload behavior, or require neighboring clauses/context; they are retained rather than guessed.
- BGM/flavor clauses are out of damage-runtime scope and should not be promoted into the damage engine.
